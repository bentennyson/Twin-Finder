The encoder was converted to frozen TorchScript for offline CPU inference by
research/prepare_encoder.py. Its pretrained weights were not fine-tuned.
Mean pooling, L2 normalization and a 256-token limit are preserved. Conversion
checks and artifact hashes are in encoder_manifest.json.
base_classifier.json is the separately trained project-specific logistic
matching classifier; its training code and benchmark provenance are supplied.
